## 1. Executive Summary (Short Pitch)
**Character Count:** 680 characters (Well within 1,000 char limit)

YouthForge is an autonomous multi-agent venture validation studio designed to empower young builders to transform raw, unpolished ideas into evidence-backed, reality-checked ventures. Rather than operating as a chatbot that generates ungrounded advice, YouthForge orchestrates five specialist agents: Spark (coordinator), Scout (research squad pulling live citations from Dubai RTA and market indices), Judge (calibrated 0–100 feasibility rubric and critic), Smith (actionable 1–14 day experiment planner), and Trail (milestone evolution). Every plan is sealed with a canonical SHA-256 digest, providing verifiable proof without requiring crypto wallets or exposing minor PII.

---

## 2. Problem Statement & Youth Opportunity
Young innovators across the UAE and globally have high creative ambition but face three critical barriers:
1. **The Validation Void:** Ideas stall because founders don't know how to conduct rigorous market, competitor, and regulatory research.
2. **Chatbot Hallucination Trap:** LLM chatbots produce sycophantic, over-optimistic praise without empirical citations or calibrated feasibility critiques.
3. **The Proof Deficit:** Young builders lack credible, verifiable records to demonstrate progress, milestone execution, and idea provenance to incubators, mentors, and grant juries.

---

## 3. How the 5-Agent Pipeline Operates (Autonomous Agent Proof)
YouthForge is built around an explicit, bounded state graph rather than a single prompt:
* **Spark (Intake & Coordination):** Analyzes natural language inputs, clarifies ambiguities with targeted chips, and plans 4–5 empirical research tasks.
* **Scout (Research Squad):** Dispatches parallel retrieval calls, extracts primary sources (e.g., Dubai government strategies, WAM news, unit economics data), and ties claims to verified citations.
* **Judge (Feasibility & Critic):** Evaluates a deterministic 5-dimension rubric (Market Demand 25%, Competitive Edge 20%, Youth Team Feasibility 25%, Regulatory Simplicity 15%, Evidence Quality 15%). Delivers honest verdict bands (Forge Ahead 70–100, Refine & Retry 45–69, Rethink 0–44) and an active risk register.
* **Smith (Action Planner):** Forges the first 5 next actions (1–14 day low-cost experiments) and computes an immutable canonical SHA-256 plan digest.
* **Trail (Progress & Evolution):** Tracks user evidence notes, re-evaluates rubric dimensions, and mints versioned plans (v1 → v2) preserving full audit history.

## 5. Youth Privacy & Security Architecture
* **Zero Minor PII:** No collection or storage of minor birth dates, national ID scans, or school rosters.
* **Privacy by Design:** Private venture ideas are never written to public blockchains or third-party training feeds.
