# YouthForge Originality & Intellectual Property Hygiene Statement

> Authored per Section 10 of the competition build specification.

---

## 1. Original Concept & Architecture
YouthForge is an independently architected and authored multi-agent system designed specifically for young founders participating in the Dubai Chambers Create AI Agents Championship.

- **Originality in Conception:** Unlike generic single-prompt chatbots or templated startup generators, YouthForge implements a five-agent collaborative graph (Spark, Scout, Judge, Smith, Trail) with explicit, bounded loops, calibrated 0-100 rubric evaluations, untrusted data sanitization, verifiable SHA-256 canonical digests, and an ongoing milestone progress tracker.
- **Originality in UI/UX:** The visual direction ("The Idea Forge Editorial") was developed uniquely around the theme of tempering and testing early ideas with real-world evidence, featuring custom SVG motifs, typography, and live telemetry feeds.
- **No Mechanical Imitation:** No third-party codebases, commercial SaaS applications, or competitor solutions have been cloned, vendored, or mechanically reproduced.

---

## 2. Dependency Provenance
All third-party libraries used in the system are open-source packages under standard permissive licenses (MIT, Apache-2.0, ISC) and are explicitly recorded in `docs/PROVENANCE.md`.
