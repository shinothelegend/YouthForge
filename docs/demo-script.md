## 00:00 - 01:30 | Introduction & Problem Context (90 seconds)
* **Action:** Slide 1 & 2 on screen.
* **Speaker:**
  "Judges, government leaders, and fellow innovators: every young founder in Dubai has high creative ambition. But when a student enters an idea into a traditional AI chatbot, they get empty praise and hallucinated numbers. That is not an AI agent. An AI agent reasons, plans, uses tools, checks facts, and stays with you as you learn.
